Vercel Breach Tied to Context AI Hack Exposes Limited Customer Credentials

April 20, 2026 Tech News Team 6 min read
Breaches Threats

Vercel reported a security breach caused by the compromise of Context.ai, a third-party AI tool used by an employee. Attackers gained access through a compromised OAuth token, allowing them to take over the employee's Google Workspace account and access parts of Vercel's systems, including some non-sensitive environment variables. Sensitive data remained protected due to encryption, and only a limited number of customers were affected, though some credentials were exposed. The attacker is considered highly skilled, and investigations are ongoing with cybersecurity firms. The breach may have started from malware (Lumma Stealer) infecting a Context.ai employee. Vercel and Context.ai have both implemented security improvements and advised users to enable multi-factor authentication, review activity, and rotate credentials. This incident highlights growing risks from OAuth tokens and third-party integrations in modern supply chains. Read More
