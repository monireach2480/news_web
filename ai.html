EU Passes AI Governance Act: New Regulations Take Effect April 2026

April 12, 2026 Tech News Team 8 min read
Ethics Compliance

The European Union's comprehensive AI Governance Act has officially taken effect this month, establishing strict requirements for high-risk AI systems. The regulations mandate transparency, accountability, and bias testing for AI systems used in critical sectors including healthcare, criminal justice, and employment. Companies deploying AI in EU member states must now implement documented compliance procedures and conduct regular audits. Violations can result in fines up to €10 million or 4% of global annual turnover. Tech companies including Microsoft, Google, and Meta have already begun restructuring their AI deployment practices to meet the new standards.
